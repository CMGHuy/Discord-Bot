## Observations

(a) **m never binds.** The disaster stop `max(level - m*ATR14, entry*0.98)` lands on the 2% cap for every m in {0.5, 1.0, 1.5}: 0 of 8090 rows differ across m. The six cells are therefore effectively two (b=0 and b=0.25), and the identical dExpR, intervals and MDE across m is that, not a bug.

(b) **The acceptance exit barely fires.** It fired twice among 2815 eligible rows (8090 rows less 5275 not_eligible), and only in the b=0 cells; the b=0.25 cells recorded none. Eligible levels sit within 2% of entry while ATR14 is about 2-6% of price, so the bar that closes through the level usually trades through the 2% cap first, and the intrabar stop (checked first) wins.

(c) **dExpR is a small positive R-unit and stop-widening effect, not significant.** +0.0043R with a 95% interval of [-0.0056, +0.0170], p=0.2408 (b=0) / 0.2427 (b=0.25). Arm Z's 1R is the entry-to-disaster-stop distance, larger than the old stop's, so the same price move is a smaller R; the 42 loss->win flips, no win->loss flips, and the median planned RR falling from 2.500 to 2.389 are what a wider stop does, not evidence that a close exit adds edge. Not significant, so no claim either way.

(d) The win-rate floor and volume clauses pass everywhere; only `expectancy_gain` fails.
