## Reading notes (disclosure, no gate changed)

- **A bullish is not a filter.** At `b = 0.1` it keeps 814 of the reference's 815 trades (drop rate 0.1%); N is not monotone in `b` (0.25 has 860, more than the reference) because dropping a signal changes which later signals `one_at_a_time` admits.
- **ExpR is measured in R, and R is the stop distance.** A tighter stop shrinks the denominator: at `b = 0.1` A bullish reads ExpR 0.818 against the reference's 0.222 while its win rate is *lower* (33.8% vs 36.8%). The pre-registered tier passes it on ExpR and the bootstrap lower bound (0.363), and the outcome is scored as registered, but the ExpR gain is not a like-for-like dollar gain. VALIDATION will show whether it survives out of sample.
- **Outlier fold.** A bullish's 2016 fold is ExpR 2.977 on N=29. 9 of 11 folds are positive, and the Stage 2 verdict counts signs, so it does not lean on that fold.
- **C bullish** had a Stage 1 Tier 2 winner (`0.618`, ExpR 0.243, lower bound 0.092, N=1199) yet only 6 of 11 folds were positive (needs >= 2/3 of 11 qualifying folds, i.e. 8). Stage 2 is the pre-registered arbiter; it does not proceed.
- **Bearish sides are thin.** A bearish has 3 unselected folds and only 2 qualifying; C bearish has 3 unselected and 1 qualifying, most test years below N=15 (see Stage 0 thin years). Neither has a Stage 1 winner.
