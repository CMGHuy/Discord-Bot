### Deviation from the plan: what leg b reads (partner decision)

The spec assumed the dev `data/backtest_cache/` reaches back to 2000. It starts 2018-06-01, so truncating it at 2016-01-01 dropped nothing: the first leg-b snapshot hashed equal to leg a (`d60ed4bb...`) and was deleted. The partner chose: leg a stays today's cache; leg b is `data/backtest_cache_ext/` truncated to 2016-01-01. Consequences for reading the table:

- **b -> c isolates the vendor** as intended: both start in 2016.
- **a -> b is not a pure warm-up effect.** It is today's cache (2018-06 start) against a 2016 start, and it also carries any difference between the two yfinance downloads (fetched at different dates, so auto-adjust factors can drift). The drift is small: SPY's 2020-01-02 open is 294.18244867 in leg a and 294.18241827 in leg b.
- Leg a has **no look-back at all** at the start of the fold-train window (2018-06-01), so its trade count there is lower than b and c (3367 vs 4155 and 4095). That is warm-up, not strategy behaviour.
