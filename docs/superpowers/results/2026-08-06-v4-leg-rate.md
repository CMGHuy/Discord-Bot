# V4 Step 3 — the post-fix leg rate

**Date:** 2026-08-06
**Task:** plan v8 V4 Step 3 ("re-measure the leg rate after ≥1 week of live
sessions on the fix")
**Data:** live `/opt/swing-bot/data/{trades,plans}.json`, 558 closed records

---

## The prediction being tested

V4 Step 2 fixed the race that made scale-out dead in the live log: the legacy
SL/TP loops in `performance.py` closed 100% of a v2 position the first time
price touched TP1, so the manager's `tp1_partial` hit `append_leg_by_plan` on
an already-closed trade and silently no-opped. Step 3 pre-registered the check
and, unusually, also pre-registered the fallback:

> Expect roughly *every* v2 win from here on to carry two legs. If it doesn't,
> the remaining suspect is the plan/trade linkage itself (see **V45**), not the
> exit model.

## Result — the fix works, and the residual is exactly the named suspect

| cohort | v2 wins | with ≥2 legs |
|---|---|---|
| pre-fix (opened < 2026-07-31) | 212 | **7 (3.3%)** |
| post-fix, incl. the fix's own day | 36 | 20 (55.6%) |
| **post-fix, clean (opened ≥ 2026-08-01)** | **18** | **16 (88.9%)** |

The middle row is not the answer: the fix landed *during* the 2026-07-31
session, so trades opened that day straddle it — 13 of the 15 zero-leg wins in
that row opened on 07-31, and 8 carry `close_reason = "auto (price monitor)"`
with `exit_price` exactly equal to `take_profit`, which is the pre-fix
signature. Excluding that straddling day, **3.3% → 88.9%**.

**Both remaining exceptions are orphans.** RKLB and STX, opened 2026-08-04 at
20:24 and 20:36, closed 08-05. Their `plan_id`s (`c7aa1f86`, `cd38e343`) exist
**nowhere in plans.json** — RKLB has 9 plan records and STX 19, none matching.
The split is total:

| | plan record present | plan record missing |
|---|---|---|
| wins with 2 legs | **16 of 16** | 0 |
| wins with 0 legs | 0 | **2 of 2** |

That is `manager_owns_target()` behaving exactly as documented: it hands the
target side over *only* while the plan is one `PlanManager.poll()` will
actually see, because a trade whose plan is missing or terminal is watched by
nobody. Plan gone → legacy loop keeps the target → full-size close at TP1 with
no legs.

**Where the orphans came from.** Both opened inside the 2026-08-04 outage
recovery window (backups at 18:43, 18:45, 19:05; `data/{plans,trades,account,
journal}.json` restored from the pre-gap backup). A restore that returns
plans.json to an earlier point than trades.json leaves exactly this: trades
referencing plans the restored file never had. Historical damage from that
incident, not an ongoing defect — no trade opened after it shows the pattern.

## Standing monitor

The failure is silent — nothing errors when a runner is lost, the trade just
settles at TP1 — so `live_cohort_report.py` gained a **Legs** cohort. A win
appearing under `0 legs` is the alarm.

**That cohort is a diagnostic, not evidence.** Its expectancy column is close
to tautological: a 2-leg trade banked TP1 and is therefore a win by
construction, and a 1-leg trade is the synthesized `fraction=1.0` leg of a
pre-TP1 loss. The 2026-08-01+ read (2+ legs: WR 100%, +1.180R; 1 leg: WR 0%,
−1.783R) restates win/loss and must **not** be cited as measuring what
scale-out earns. The only load-bearing number is the count under `0 legs`.

## Verdict

V4 Step 3 closes. The exit-model fix is confirmed live at 88.9%, and 100% of
the shortfall is attributable to plan/trade linkage — the suspect Step 3
named in advance — rather than to the exit model. The linkage itself stays
open under **V45**, whose orphan backfill is a human call needing the live
`journal.json`; this adds two more known orphans (RKLB, STX) to that list.
