**Read the "drop rate" column with the ΔExpR column, not alone.** Several cells that show a
positive ΔExpR also drop over a third of their trades (Support/Resistance 35.2%, MA Ribbon
41.2%, EMA Crossover bullish 48.9%) — the structural stop is filtering hard, not just
sizing differently. The four proceeding cells keep drop rates at 13-16%, except
Support/Resistance at 35.2%: its ExpR gain (+0.107) and Tier 2 bootstrap lower bound
(+0.281) are both comfortably positive, but the reader should treat the passing arm as
measurably more selective than the baseline, not as "the same trades, priced differently."

Both Elliott Wave cells have **zero** in-scope trades: every structural stop for this
strategy exceeds its horizon's `max_risk_pct`, so the in-arm dropped every trade the
out-arm took. This is "in scope = drop, never cap" working as designed, not a data error —
confirmed by re-reading `data/v104_collect_A_elliott-wave-{bullish,bearish}.json`, whose
`arms.in` arrays are empty.
