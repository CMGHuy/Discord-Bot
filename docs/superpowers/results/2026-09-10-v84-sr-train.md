## Observations

This confirms R24's own concern, flagged in its implementation report: "a
well-tested level ... racks up touch counts very quickly ... `min_level_touches`
in the 1-2 range may turn out to be a very weak filter." That prediction
holds exactly — N barely moves (247 -> 236 -> 230 -> 226, a ~9% reduction
across the whole grid) and win rate does not improve at all, drifting
slightly *worse* (45.7% baseline -> 44.9%/44.3%/44.2%). Unlike RSI
Divergence's sharp K=3/K=4 collapse, this is a filter with essentially no
teeth on this population: most breakouts in the TRAIN sample already clear a
1-touch bar, so the gate rarely excludes anything, and what little it does
exclude isn't disproportionately bad. The flat, stable plateau across all
three K values (is_plateau: True) is consistent with this — a real, weak-but-
non-noisy effect, not a fluke.
