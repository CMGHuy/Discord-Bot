## Observations

- **Arm S trades a higher win rate for a lower expectancy.** Tighter structural stops convert 60–165
  baseline losers into wins (only 1 win turned loss), lifting win rate up to +1.26pp, but the planned RR
  falls from 2.50 to about 2.0 and pooled ExpR drops by 0.026–0.033R. The gain on the loser side does not
  pay for the shorter reward.
- **1R is larger in arm S**, so the same price move is a smaller R. A ΔExpR in R is a ΔExpR in dollars only
  under fixed-dollar sizing (spec § Why); this record does not claim a dollar result either way.
- **Live would cancel fewer fills than baseline**: `fill_beyond_ceiling` falls (1197 → 1017/713/592), so the
  stop-ceiling cancellation is not what drives the loss.
- The effect is broad rather than horizon-led: per-horizon N is flat and no single horizon was selected out.
- The win-rate clause is not the problem (PASS in all cells); the failure is purely expectancy.
