## Per-horizon breakdown (additional measurement, not in the plan's script -- see Phase A final review, Important #2)

The Phase A whole-branch review flagged a design concern: the confluence tolerance is a fixed multiple of ATR14, while the gap between a tested Fibonacci level and the nearest Rolling S/R level tends to grow with a horizon's swing-lookback window, so the filter could act as a de-facto short-horizon-only mask rather than a genuine cross-horizon confluence filter. **Honest statement of what any later PASS would describe:** since bearish is already closed at this stage, only bullish carries forward. Given the table above, a bullish PASS at any grid tolerance would describe an almost-entirely-2w population (with a small 4w contribution), not a cross-horizon Fibonacci result. This will be stated explicitly in the V102-6 pre-registration, per the ruling recorded in the plan's SDD ledger after the Phase A review -- it is disclosure, not a reason to change the grid or the filter definition at this stage.
