## Observations

- The gate fires: it removed 150 of 493 pilot trades and 1300–2400 of 10541 fold-train rows, so the instrument is exercised for the strategy scope.
- The effect on win rate is real but small (+0.13 to +0.41pp) and below what this sample can resolve (MDE 1.7 to 3.0pp). Expectancy falls in every cell (−0.017R to −0.040R).
- The clause-6 reading was not a gate at this stage, but it is recorded: the trades the predicate flags have a positive expectancy of about +0.25R to +0.29R, and a win rate within about 1pp of the retained trades. A level 1R or closer is not a reliable sign of a loser for these strategies in this window.
