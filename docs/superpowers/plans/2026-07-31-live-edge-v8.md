# Live Edge & PnL Repair — Implementation Plan (v8)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Execute in order V1→V35.

**Goal:** Make the live paper book profitable by (a) guaranteeing every winning trade banks **≥2.5%**, (b) cutting the two cohorts that produce ~100% of the losses, and (c) retraining the whole parameter surface on the **25 years of daily history already on disk** instead of the bull-heavy 2020–2023 window that produced the current settings. Then close every live-verification task that plans v2–v7 deferred for lack of a live server.

**Architecture:** A new `MIN_TARGET_PCT` floor is applied inside `plan_engine`'s four sizing helpers so TP1 can never be smaller than a fixed percentage of entry, paired with a *reachability screen* in the scan pipeline that refuses to alert setups whose structure cannot support that move. The gatekeeper (built, tested, and currently switched off) becomes the loss-side filter. Training moves from `data/backtest_cache/` — which does not exist on the live server — onto a no-network bridge from the existing `market_data/daily/` corpus.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, pandas/numpy, pytest, existing `scripts/` backtest + walk-forward harness. No new dependencies. No ML in the live path.

### Root cause is structural

`plan_engine.py:170-197` computes `TP1 = entry ± risk_distance × rr`, with `rr` from
`STRATEGY_RR_OVERRIDE` (`strategy_types.py:217-232`, all values 0.30–0.40) floored at
`RR_FLOOR = 0.30` (`plan_engine.py:31`). The stop is the *full* `risk_distance` (1R).
A win therefore banks ~0.35R while a loss costs ~1R **by construction**:

- median designed TP1 distance = **0.85%** (v2 confluence path: **0.77%**)
- median designed stop distance = **2.19%**
- median designed R:R = **0.35**

The median winning trade *cannot arithmetically* reach 2%. This was deliberate —
tight targets buying a high win rate (`strategy_types.py:210-216` targets 80%+ WR) —
and is recorded as a frozen constant in `docs/claude/backtest-methodology.md:15`.
Live, the win rate bought (55.6%) is far below the win rate required (63.2%).

Journal exit efficiency confirms the waste: median **0.24**. The median winner had
**0.92R** of favourable excursion available; we took **0.35R**.

| legacy `S/R Confluence` (untiered, no badge, `source=None`) | 154 | **26.0%** | +4.10% | −2.34% | **−103.0%** |

One legacy strategy is **72% of all damage**. Tier A and B are already profitable.
The gatekeeper — the built, tested lever for exactly this — is **off**
(`GATE_ENABLED=false`, `GATE_MODE=inform`, `config.py:566-574`).

## Risks carried into execution

1. **Survivorship bias worsens with the 1999 window.** The watchlist is *today's*
   megacap winners (NVDA's 1999 bar is $0.0376 split-adjusted). Training long-biased
   strategies on 25 years of survivors overstates bullish edge. `STRATEGY_GATES`
   (`strategy_types.py:246-263`) already forces Fibonacci and RSI bullish-only — that
   may itself be the artifact. V21 exists to expose this and must be willing to
   report that the long-side edge is not real.
2. **The permutation stop rule already fired once.** Gatekeeper G100 found `p ≥ 0.05`
   for *every* strategy. If V22 fires again, the correct outcome is to record "no
   demonstrable edge" and stop, not to keep tuning.
3. **MFE-based projections are optimistic by ~0.3R** (measured: the method projects
   +0.14R for tier C, which actually lost money live). Projections are directional
   only; V24 and V26–V29 settle the real effect.
4. **Volume collapse.** Tier-A/B-only cuts 475 → ~138 trades per 26 days; the
   reachability screen cuts further. V12 measures combined survival *before*
   enforcement. If forward-test N gets too small, drop the tier floor to C-with-floor
   rather than abandoning the gate.
5. **Unfreezing rr invalidates the badge registry**, which the 80%-WR validation
   underpins. Badges are stale between V9 and V25.
