**Goal:** Turn a P&L calendar day into a full admin page at `/calendar/:date` (day-vs-normal context, a strategy/horizon/direction/exit breakdown, rich trade cards with price sparklines), keep the drawer as the quick preview with an "Open full day" button, and put the calendar's month and filters in the URL.

**Architecture:** Pure additions in `swingbot/core/analytics/pnl_calendar.py` (fuller rows from the prerequisite's `expand_realized_legs` parts plus the parent trade, `day_context`, `day_breakdown`, and `day_totals` lifted out of the radon-D day route), a renamed public `metrics.exit_reason_bucket` with a text-only half for leg reasons, and a new pure `swingbot/core/analytics/day_sparklines.py` that windows cached `market_data/` daily frames. `GET /calendar/pnl/day` gains `context` and `breakdown`; a new `GET /calendar/pnl/day/sparklines` serves the series. The Angular side adds a `calendar/:date` route with a `CalendarDayStore`, a pure `month-context.ts` (rank, MTD, curve, prev/next), a new tick-and-dot sparkline component and a trade card; the calendar store reads and writes `month`/`strategy`/`horizon` query params.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, Flask, pandas, pytest via `scripts/dev/testrun.py`, radon; Angular (signals, ngrx signalStore) + vitest via `ng test`.

- **Day route contract kept:** `GET /calendar/pnl/day` keeps every existing field, its meaning, and the 404 on a day with no rows. New fields only: `context`, `breakdown`.
- **Null is a measured answer, never 0:** every missing input yields `null` (`return_pct` null on missing/zero entry or missing exit; `held_hours` null when either stamp is missing or `closed_at < opened_at`; `win_rate` null for a group with no win/loss rows; null-net days excluded from percentile and averages; a null-net-R day excluded from the R deltas only).
- **One day-net rule:** a day's net P&L / net R is `day_summary(day, rows)["net_pnl_amount"|"net_r"]` (the calendar cell's rule). D2 and D3 call it; no second definition.
- **Reference set = the other trading days:** the day is excluded from its own percentile and both means. `percentile = (strictly below + 0.5 * ties) / other days * 100`, `null` when fewer than `PERCENTILE_MIN_DAYS = 20` other days with non-null net P&L. Page-side: below `PERCENTILE_BAND_DAYS = 60` show a third (bottom/middle/top) with "small sample"; below 20 hide with "Only N trading days so far — percentile hidden".
- **No third copy of leg pricing:** D1 reads the part's own fields (`entry`, `exit_price`, `stop_loss`, `take_profit`, `target2`, `shares`, `opened_at`, `closed_at`); `api_v1/trades._held_hours` and `_row_from_leg` are not touched or imported.
- **`metrics._exit_reason_bucket` is renamed `exit_reason_bucket`** with every caller (metrics.py's two, `tests/analytics/test_metrics_exit_reasons.py`) in one commit, **no alias**; its trade-level output is unchanged.
- **Leg R is not additive:** each leg row's `r_multiple` is `metrics.r_multiple(part)`; nothing on the page sums leg R as a trade R. The one leg invariant claimed: a trade's leg `pnl_amount`s sum to its `realized_pnl_amount`.
- **Sparklines read only `data_store.load_normalized(ticker, "daily")`** — never a network fetch, never `backtest_cache`, never `load_from_disk`. A spot metal (`spot_metals.is_spot_metal`, checked **before** any load), a missing file, or an empty window → `null`. Window: 3 bars before the entry bar to 3 bars after the exit bar, never later; entry bar = first bar on/after the UTC date of `opened_at`; exit bar = last bar on/before the row's `day`. Ticks are the recorded prices; all ticks `null` when `entry` is outside the window's `[min Low, max High]`. `stale` when the frame's last bar is before the exit bar's date. Caps: rows in `closed_at` order, first 50; a series over 400 bars keeps the last 400; either cut sets `truncated: true`. Only the slice is kept.

## Review Focus

1. **A day whose rows all have a null P&L** (manual closes without an amount): its own net is null, so `context.percentile` and both deltas are `null` and the page renders `—`, never `0` or a crash. Pinned in CD3 (`test_a_day_with_a_null_net_gets_null_context`) and CD12.
2. **A leg whose reason is missing, unknown, or whose `leg_index` overruns `trade["legs"]`**: `exit_reason` is `"other"`, never a KeyError/IndexError. Pinned in CD2 (`test_a_leg_with_no_or_unknown_reason_buckets_other`).
3. **A malformed or impossible `:date`** (`2026-02-30`, `abc`, `2026-8-3`): empty state, no HTTP request at all. Pinned in CD8 (`isIsoDate`) and CD12.
4. **A filtered URL whose day has no rows** (404 from the day route): empty state with working prev/next taken from the month response. Pinned in CD9 and CD12.
5. **A ticker whose cache ends before the exit, or has no file, or is a spot metal**: `stale: true` / `null` series; the card shows the series stopping early with a "cache ends <date>" note, or `—`. Pinned in CD6 and CD10.
