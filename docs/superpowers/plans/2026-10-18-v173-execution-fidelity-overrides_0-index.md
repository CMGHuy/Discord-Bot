**Goal:**

The bot trades its own plans mechanically; the one place a human changes an outcome is the admin **close** verb (`trade_commands.py:_close_plan`). Production on 2026-10-17 held 563 CLOSED plans, 36 of them closed `manual`: about 21 individual overrides between 2026-08-14 and 2026-09-22, plus 15 from a single bulk clear on 2026-09-30 20:54â€“20:55 UTC. Nobody can say whether those overrides helped, for two reasons: a manual close stores no exit price (only `{status, reason, at}`), and a bulk clear is recorded exactly like a decision. Douglas's point, that a trader who takes profit early or moves stops is trading an unmeasured system, has never been checked against this book.

Once this ships, every manual close stamps its exit price, the price's source, the quote time and the bar-cache date onto its `CLOSED` transition, and a bulk clear says `manual_bulk`. The 15 historical bulk closes are re-tagged by a pinned-id Alembic data revision. A pure replay module re-runs each overridden plan through the v2 exit engine from its real fill bar (never resuming mid-trade), on bars trimmed to the last completed session, with indicators computed on the real bars and only the exit checks seeing the clamped override bar; it books both arms through the same `costs.book` call shape and reports per override the R and dollars the engine would have realised had the plan been left alone. A report script (run on the VM), `GET /api/v1/analytics/override-fidelity` and an "Overrides vs engine" panel on the Analytics Execution tab show the rows, the descriptive totals and a verdict badge.

The verdict is a frozen pre-registration, and the plan panel of 2026-10-19 showed that where it is stored and who triggers it decide whether it stays frozen. So the authority is a new Postgres table `preregistration_verdicts` (primary key `ledger_id`: a second insert is impossible), and the only evaluator is a daily bot-scheduler job after the US close that evaluates the first time the floors hold. Nobody runs it by hand, so nobody picks the moment after seeing the sums. The script and the API only read the table; `preregistration-ledger.jsonl` receives the same row by a documented mirror commit, as the record.

It leaves alone how a manual close settles (`legs_realized`, `r_multiple`, equity, every existing analytics figure), every signal, gate, stop, size and badge, the exit engine's behaviour for every existing caller (the new `indicator_df` keyword defaults to today's frame, byte-identical), and the live plan lifecycle: no ghost plans, no `resume` in `exit_sim_v2`, no eToro code, no Discord verb.

Success is measured by integrity, not lift: every new manual close carries a four-field `override` stamp, and a failed stamp shows up as an `unstamped` exclusion rather than silently leaving the denominator; the 15 pinned rows read `manual_bulk` on production (the deploy step fails otherwise); the report runs on the VM over the real book and prints every override with exactly one status; and the verdict stays `WAITING` until its frozen floors (30 stamped individual resolved rows over 10 ISO weeks, none open, exclusions â‰¤ 20%) hold, after which the daily job writes it once to `preregistration_verdicts` as `v173-override-fidelity`, it is mirrored into the jsonl ledger, and it is never recomputed.

**Architecture:** Stamping lives in `swingbot/admin/api_v1/trade_commands.py` (a never-raising `_override_stamp` helper, `record_transition(..., extra=)` from `plan_types.py`). Replay and descriptive rows live in the new pure module `swingbot/core/analytics/override_fidelity.py`, which adapts a live plan to `walk_v2` through a new public `exit_sim_v2.fill_bar_exit`, the additive `ExitResult.tp1_index` and the new optional `walk_v2(..., indicator_df=None)` keyword. The frozen verdict rule, the stored-record shape, the jsonl mirror row and the I/O-free `build_report` live in the new pure `swingbot/core/analytics/override_verdict.py`. The stored verdict lives in the Postgres table `preregistration_verdicts` (`schema.py` + Alembic `v173_002`, chained after the data revision `v173_001`) behind `swingbot/core/db/repositories/preregistration_verdicts.py` (insert-once via `on_conflict_do_nothing` on the primary key). The only I/O glue is the new `swingbot/core/analytics/override_io.py` (`load_inputs`, `stored_verdict`, `run_daily_check`), called by the daily loop `override_verdict_check` in `swingbot/commands/scanning/loops.py` (`_always_on_loops()`, modelled on `gate_counterfactual_resolve`), by the read-only report script and by the API route. The SPA gets a store slice on `AnalyticsStore` and a standalone `sb-override-fidelity-panel` mounted in `execution.ts`.
