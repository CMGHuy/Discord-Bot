**Goal:** Under the v2 instrument, every price-vs-level comparison the exit simulator makes goes through a new `instrument/fills.py` (gap-through at the open in both directions, next-open market entries that cancel on a gap through the stop, stop-first), and every v2 trade books commission and slippage once, in R, through a new `instrument/costs.py`. `resolve("v2")` carries spec section 2's values. v1 stays the default and byte-identical.

**Architecture:** One dispatch seam. `exit_sim.simulate_exit(..., instrument=None)` gains a keyword; a spec whose `version != "v1"` is handed to `simulate_exit_v2` in a new module, `swingbot/core/planning/exit_sim_v2.py`. That module reuses exit_sim's pure helpers unchanged (`ExitResult`, `_not_triggered`, `_no_trade`, `_hold_cap_bars`, `_is_compression`, `_limit_unfilled`, `acceptance_exit`, `runner_floor`, `_chandelier_ratchet`, `_structure_update`, `_runner_timeout`, `runner_structure_frame`) and replaces only the compare-and-fill lines with `fills.py` calls. It records raw legs (`fraction`, `exit_price`, `reason`, `kind`) and books them once through `costs.book`, which returns the net per-leg `r` and `r_total`. Consumers keep reading `ExitResult.r_total` and need no change. No v1 function body in `exit_sim.py` is edited (spec rule 1), so the v1 golden and every older exit test stay identical by construction. The instrument is threaded, as an optional keyword defaulting to `None` (= v1), into `_replay_live_constructor`, both arms engines, `run_arm` and `backtest_wf`. Under v2, `BacktestTrade.entry_date` is the fill bar's date and a new `BacktestTrade.signal_date` carries the signal bar's date (the cross-plan contract with v158).

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, pandas/numpy, pytest (+xdist via `scripts/dev/testrun.py`), radon, `tests/helpers.py:make_ohlcv` synthetic tapes, the frozen OHLCV fixtures in `tests/fixtures/ohlcv/` (`tests/fixtures/ohlcv_parity.py`).

- **The seam (controller decision 2):** `simulate_exit(df, signal_index, plan, *, scale_out=False, max_holding_days=None, instrument=None)`. Its first statement dispatches `instrument is not None and instrument.version != "v1"` to `exit_sim_v2.simulate_exit_v2` through a function-local import (`exit_sim_v2` imports from `exit_sim`, so a module-level import would cycle). `simulate_exit` goes from cc 11 to 12.
- **`fills.py` imports nothing from `swingbot` except `contract`.** It must not import `plan_manager` (that module imports `exit_sim`: a cycle) nor `planning.lifecycle`. - **Fill rules under v2 (spec section 2 plus controller decisions 3 and 7):**
  - *Market* entries fill at bar `signal_index + 1`'s open. An open at or beyond the stop cancels the trade: `_not_triggered("gap_through_stop")`, outcome `not_triggered`, never counted. No next bar in the frame: `_not_triggered()` (reason-less). On that entry bar the stop is checked (stop-first) and the target is ignored; the walk then starts at the next bar, as every walk does.
  - *Limit* entries: fill at `min(open, limit)` bull / `max` bear (today's rule). A fill at or beyond the stop is still a scratch exiting at the fill price, but under v2 its leg reason is `entry_gap_scratch` (the string `gap_through_stop` is reserved for the market cancel). The fill bar is checked for the stop, as today.
  - *Stop* entries: fill at `max(open, trigger)` bull / `min` bear (today's rule). The compression short keeps its risk-cap cancel (`"risk_cap"`, evaluated on the actual fill) and its fill-bar check, with the same `entry_gap_scratch` rename. Under v2, every other stop entry also checks the stop on its fill bar, unlike v1's walk, which never checks the fill bar. This is a controller decision: the bar's path after the trigger is unknown, so the stop is checked first, which is conservative. The target is not checked on the fill bar.
  - *Exits:* a stop of any kind (initial, breakeven, runner floor, chandelier) fills at `min(open, stop)` bull / `max(open, stop)` bear; TP1 and TP2 fill at `max(open, target)` bull / `min(open, target)` bear. When the instrument's `fill_model.gap_through` is false, both fill at the level.
  - *Same bar:* stop before target, everywhere. A trailing stop computed from bar `j`'s close is checked from bar `j + 1` on (already true of the reused `_chandelier_ratchet` / `_structure_update`).
  - *Outcome labels* stay event-based: a stop before the breakeven move is `loss`, after it `scratch`, a TP1 touch `win`, whatever the net `r` after gaps and costs. `r` is price-derived, never the v1 constants −1.0 / 0.0.
- **Cost rules under v2 (spec section 2 table, controller decisions 5 and 6):**
  - `resolve("v2").cost_model == CostModel(commission_per_share=0.0, slippage_bps=5.0, stop_slippage_bps=10.0)`.
  - The entry is slipped against the trader at `slippage_bps` (a bull buys higher). Exit legs of kind `"stop"` slip at `stop_slippage_bps`; kinds `"target"` and `"close"` (timeout, acceptance, stall, runner progress stall at the open) slip at `slippage_bps`.
  - Risk basis: `risk = |slipped entry − stop_loss|`. Leg `r = ((slipped exit − slipped entry) * sign − 2 * commission_per_share) / risk`. Leg `r` is rounded to 3 dp; `r_total = round(Σ fraction * unrounded r, 3)`: costs are applied to unrounded values and rounded once.
  - `ExitResult.entry_price` and every leg's `exit_price` stay the **raw** fill prices; costs show only in `r`.
  - The v1 config fields `SLIPPAGE_BPS`, `COMMISSION_PER_TRADE`, `COMMISSION_RISK_BASIS` and `edge/frictions.py` are v1-only and untouched; v2 costs come from the contract only.
- **Contract ownership (controller decision 8, cross-plan with v158):** this plan edits only `FillModel`/`CostModel` (no new fields are needed: the existing three of each carry every rule above), the new `V2_FILLS`/`V2_COSTS` constants, `_SPECS["v2"]` and the module docstring. It never adds an `InstrumentSpec` field (v158 appends span/universe/fold fields, keyword-with-default, at the end).
- **Dates (controller decision 4, cross-plan with v158):** `BacktestTrade` gains `signal_date: str | None = None`, appended last; `__post_init__` sets it to `entry_date` when `None`, so every v1 row carries `signal_date == entry_date` with no edit to the v1 loop's constructor calls. The new key would change the bytes of every `dataclasses.asdict(BacktestTrade)` record, so the golden renderer and the v131 witness drop it after asserting `signal_date == entry_date` (the v131 `limit_orders` precedent in `golden.py`). `ArmTrade` is not changed: the arms engines keep stamping the signal date.

Callers not listed (`backtest_scenarios`, `armed_replay`, `acceptance_replay`, `fvg_diagnostic`, the `measure_*` scripts, reports) keep calling without an instrument, i.e. v1.
